Meet VARON

VR is the short symbol for VARON — a fictional element representing the source of invention. Technologies orbit its nucleus while an educational particle display explores alpha, beta and gamma radiation.

PARTICLE FIELD

Three types of radiation

Each emission is randomly selected. Particle labels travel just above their trails. The page animation runs for 20 seconds, pauses for 20 seconds, then repeats.

α ALPHA

High ionizing power

Relatively heavy, positively charged helium nuclei. In real materials they have the shortest penetration range.

Ionizing powerHighest
PenetrationLowest
β BETA

Medium ionizing power

Fast electrons or positrons. Their penetration is greater than alpha, but lower than gamma.

Ionizing powerMedium
PenetrationMedium
γ GAMMA

Lowest ionizing power

High-energy electromagnetic radiation. It travels at light speed and has the greatest penetrating ability.
